FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is ThunderStudy AI free?

Yes. You get 3 free AI mind maps every day, and the ready-made CUET, SSC and Banking presets open instantly without using any credits. No credit card is needed.

What can I turn into a mind map?

A topic name, pasted notes, a PDF chapter, a photo of handwritten or printed notes (read with OCR), or a YouTube video with captions.

What is the difference between Quick and Deep?

Quick gives you a compact overview of the main branches. Deep adds more sub-branches and detail, which suits full-chapter revision.

Can I save and export my maps?

Yes. Save maps to My Maps on your device, copy the outline, or export as a PDF diagram, a study booklet, an image or a standalone HTML file.

Are there any limits on PDFs and text?

PDFs are read up to 15 pages per generation and pasted text up to 9,000 characters, which keeps generation fast. You can pick the page range yourself.
